Orca Creation was founded by a group of like-minded engineers and designers with the passion of turning ideas into practical and useful solution. We love to see great ideas being transform to products that finally deliver to the market to serve their use. This is our sense of purpose and our core mission. When our clients attain their goals, it brings us immense sense of achievement.


Our aim is to help as many people as possible to bring their ideas to reality. We believe the world is big and many ideas are yet to uncover. We look forward to partnering with you to create useful products that the world has yet to see.

BACKGROUND

Orca Creation was established in year 2005 by a group of passionate professionals with years of experience in engineering and manufacturing profession. We are in the business of product design and development, which specializes in providing mechanical solution and product industrialization services. Our team consists of engineers with mechanical design expertise and a Project Management team which has experience in managing New Product Introduction (NPI) activities.
